# VELVET VAULT

A top-down stealth heist game in a single night at a dark museum. Cross the
galleries unseen, lift the **Midnight Star** from the vault, and reach the
foyer EXIT before the alarm meter fills.

- **Guards** patrol set routes with torch vision cones. Slip into a cone and
  the alarm climbs; fill a guard's suspicion and he shouts and chases.
- **Cameras** sweep their cones back and forth and feed the same alarm.
- **Tools at a cost:** three coins. Click to toss one — nearby guards turn
  to the noise, but a spent coin is score you no longer hold.
- **Sneak** (Shift) to move slowly and shrink every eye's reach.
- Side loot — paintings, vases, bullion — is pure score. The Star is the job.

Controls: WASD / arrow keys move · Shift sneaks · mouse click throws a coin
· M mutes. Runs entirely offline from static files in `game/`
(`game/index.html` plus its ES modules under `game/`); `main.ts` is the
make.fun hand-in program, and `node serve.ts` serves the game locally on
http://localhost:8080.
